Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common passenger queries.

Online check-in opens 24 hours before your scheduled departure and closes 2 hours before. Log in with your booking reference on the SIA website or app. You can also select or change your seat during online check-in at no extra charge for Economy passengers.
Wheelchair assistance can be requested at the time of booking by selecting "Special Assistance" in the passenger details form, or by contacting SIA's call centre at (+92-21) 111-786-786 at least 48 hours before departure. You can also use the contact form on this page and select "Special Assistance" as the topic.
If your flight is delayed by more than 2 hours, SIA will provide refreshments at the airport. For delays over 5 hours, you may be entitled to alternative arrangements or a full refund under the Air Passenger Protection Regulations. Contact SIA staff at the airport or call our 24/7 helpline for real-time updates.
Full baggage allowances for Economy and Business class on domestic and international routes are listed on our Experience page. Cabin baggage is limited to 7kg for Economy and 12kg for Business. Checked baggage varies by route. Extra baggage can be purchased at time of booking for a discounted rate.
