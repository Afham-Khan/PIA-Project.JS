Accessible Travel Support

Passengers who need wheelchair support, medical assistance, or mobility guidance can request help before departure. SIA ensures all passengers travel comfortably and with dignity — regardless of ability.

Special assistance must be requested at least 48 hours before departure. Contact our support team or use the form on the Contact page.

Wheelchair Support

Airport wheelchair assistance from check-in through boarding and deplaning. Pre-request ensures availability on all routes.

Medical Assistance

Passengers with medical conditions can request oxygen, specific seating, or pre-board clearance with a doctor's note submitted in advance.

Elderly Passenger Support

Priority check-in, seating near the cabin door, and extra time for boarding are available for elderly travellers upon request.

Family Travel Support

Families with young children receive priority boarding, bassinet seats on long-haul flights, and child meal options pre-bookable at no extra charge.

HCI Principles Applied

How This Page Improves Usability

Every design decision on this page maps to a specific HCI goal. Here's how.

Groups Related Services Together Navigation

Eight service categories are displayed as a scannable visual grid rather than buried inside menus or long text lists. Passengers can identify what they need in seconds without navigating sub-pages — reducing the number of clicks to reach a task.

Uses Short Descriptions Instead of Long Text Efficiency

Each service card contains one to two sentences — enough to inform, not overwhelm. The baggage guide uses a structured table layout (label + value) rather than paragraphs, making it faster to scan for specific information before reaching the airport.

Makes Special Assistance Visible and Primary Accessibility

Special Assistance is not a footnote — it is its own full-width section with a dedicated green highlight, four clear assistance types, and a direct CTA to the contact form. Passengers who need help should never have to search harder than those who do not.

Provides Direct Actions for Common Passenger Needs Efficiency

Every service card ends with a direct CTA link — not a generic "Learn More" leading to another menu. "Book Business Class", "View Baggage Guide", "Request Assistance", and "Explore Packages" each send the user directly to the relevant action or page, completing tasks in fewer steps.
