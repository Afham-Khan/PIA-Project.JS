Who We Are

Pakistan's National Carrier

SMI International Airlines is the flag carrier of Pakistan, headquartered at Jinnah International Airport in Karachi. Since 1946, SIA has connected the people of Pakistan to each other and to the world.

Airline websites play a critical role in the passenger experience. They must support trust, enable clear decision-making, and guide users through booking without confusion — whether the user is a first-time traveller or a seasoned passenger.

Domestic Network

SIA operates direct domestic routes connecting Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Multan, Quetta, and more — keeping Pakistan connected.

International Routes

SIA serves destinations across the Middle East, UK, Europe, North America, and Asia — with flagship routes to London Heathrow, Dubai, and New York.

Digital Transformation

SIA's digital services — online booking, web check-in, mobile app, and cargo tracking — are central to a modern passenger experience that this prototype improves.

Our Journey

SIA Through the Years

From a national identity to a digital travel experience — SIA's evolution reflects Pakistan's story.

1

National Airline Identity

Established in 1946, SIA became a symbol of Pakistani national pride. The green crescent and SIA logo represent a commitment to connecting the people of Pakistan to the world with dignity.

2

Domestic & International Network

Over decades, SIA expanded from a handful of routes to a comprehensive network covering 35+ cities worldwide — domestically connecting all major Pakistani cities, and internationally reaching the Middle East, Europe, and beyond.

3

Passenger Services

SIA introduced Business Class, special meals, Umrah packages, cargo services, and special assistance programmes — building a full-service experience for diverse passenger needs including accessibility-focused support.

4

Digital Travel Experience

Today, SIA's passengers expect to book, check in, track flights, and get support online. This HCI prototype reimagines that digital experience — making every passenger task faster, clearer, and more accessible.

Our Commitments

Mission & Values

Four core values that guide every passenger experience, from the booking screen to the aircraft door.

Safety

Safety is the foundation of every flight. SIA maintains strict international aviation standards, regular aircraft maintenance, and crew training — so passengers can travel with full confidence.

Service

Warm, culturally connected service from booking to landing. SIA's cabin crew, ground staff, and digital touchpoints all reflect the spirit of Pakistani hospitality — attentive, respectful, and genuinely helpful.

Accessibility

Every passenger deserves to fly with dignity. SIA provides wheelchair support, priority boarding, medical assistance, and — in this redesign — a full digital accessibility panel so all users can navigate comfortably.

Reliability

Passengers depend on SIA for important journeys — work trips, family visits, Umrah. Reliability means accurate schedules, clear communication, and fast support when things change.

HCI Rationale

Why Redesign Matters

Airline websites are among the most complex consumer-facing products. They handle high-stakes decisions — money, travel plans, schedules — often under time pressure. Poor usability directly leads to booking errors, missed flights, and lost trust.

This prototype applies five HCI principles — accessibility, consistency, navigation, user feedback, and efficiency — to redesign the core passenger journey with a focus on usability, clarity, and inclusive design.

Read Full HCI Analysis
Reduce Confusion

Complex menus and scattered services create decision fatigue. Redesigned navigation groups related tasks logically and uses clear labels so passengers always know where they are.

Prevent Booking Mistakes

Smart form validation, city autocomplete, same-city prevention, and date constraints stop errors before they happen — rather than punishing users after submission.

Make Support Easy to Find

Special assistance, refund requests, and contact options are placed where users actually need them — prominently visible, not buried in sub-menus or footnotes.

Provide Clear Feedback

Every action gets a response: loading states, success toasts, inline validation, empty states. Passengers always know the status of their request — reducing anxiety and support calls.

Work for All Passengers

High contrast mode, adjustable font size, reduced motion, and visible skip links ensure the redesign works for users with visual, motor, or cognitive differences — not just the majority.

Academic Prototype Note

This project is an academic HCI redesign prototype submitted for a Human Computer Interaction course. It uses mock flight data and focuses entirely on usability, interaction design, and passenger task completion. It does not represent real airline operations, real payment processing, or real booking confirmations.
